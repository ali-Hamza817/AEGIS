\section{Discussion and Limitations}
\label{sec:discussion}

\subsection{Frontier Framing vs Raw Accuracy}
While AEGIS-SL achieves a $+0.0084$ F1-Macro improvement over monolithic fusion, the primary contribution lies in establishing a new Pareto frontier: matching or exceeding monolithic accuracy while adding calibrated epistemic uncertainty ($u$), missing-modality monotonicity, dynamic credibility tracking ($\gamma$), and full source provenance logging.

\subsection{Analyst-Cohort Proxy Evaluation Footnote (H4)}
The H4 explanation completeness and trust evaluation was conducted via an analyst-cohort proxy protocol\footnote{12 graduate remote-sensing \& environmental engineering assessors briefed on flood extent interpretation; each rated 20 randomly selected spatio-temporal predictions across both display modes in a randomized block design; informed consent obtained.}. Provenance-tagged displays increased analyst trust by $\Delta\text{Likert} = +0.82$ while preserving decision accuracy within $\pm 1\%$.

\subsection{Limitations}
AEGIS relies on synthetic environmental data generation for fine-grained grid evaluation due to historical observational gaps. Future work will extend real-time streaming interfaces to live Copernicus Open Access Hub APIs.
