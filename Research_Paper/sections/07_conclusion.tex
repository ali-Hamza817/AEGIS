\section{Conclusion}
\label{sec:conclusion}

We presented \textbf{AEGIS}, an evidential multi-agent decision-support framework for urban flood risk assessment based on Subjective Logic. By replacing prompt-chained LLM arbitration with a deterministic Dirichlet evidence fusion router, AEGIS achieves state-of-the-art predictive accuracy ($0.7190$ F1-Macro, $0.9310$ AUROC) while providing calibrated epistemic uncertainty, formal missing-modality safety, and transparent source auditability.
