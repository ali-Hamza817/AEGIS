\section{Conclusion}
\label{sec:conclusion}

In this paper, we introduced \textbf{AEGIS} (\textit{Agentic Evidential Geographic Intelligence for Sustainability}), a multi-agent environmental decision-support framework grounded in Subjective Logic. By replacing prompt-chained LLM arbitration with a deterministic Dirichlet evidence fusion coordinator (WBF/CCF) and implementing a 27-dimensional Hybrid Evidential Head, AEGIS resolves sensory conflict while providing calibrated epistemic uncertainty ($u=0.0957$, ECE=$0.0925$), formal missing-modality monotonicity, dynamic Brier credibility tracking ($\gamma$), and source provenance logging. Evaluated on $4,800$ spatio-temporal cell-date instances from the Brisbane, Australia 2022 flood event, AEGIS achieves state-of-the-art accuracy (\textbf{0.7190 F1-Macro}, \textbf{0.9310 AUROC}), outperforming monolithic fusion ($0.7106$ F1-Macro) and LLM-arbitrated agentic baselines ($0.2256$ F1-Macro). An Analyst-Cohort Proxy Evaluation ($N=12$) confirmed $100\%$ explanation completeness and a $+0.82$ Likert trust gain over standard SHAP feature attributions. The complete open-source codebase, synthetic dataset generator, and Leaflet interactive spatial dashboard are publicly available at \url{https://github.com/ali-Hamza817/AEGIS}.
