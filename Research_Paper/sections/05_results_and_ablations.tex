\subsection{H3 Missing Modality Uncertainty Monotonicity}
As sensory modalities are systematically dropped from $n_{\text{missing}} = 0$ to $n_{\text{missing}} = 4$, epistemic uncertainty grows strictly monotonically:
\begin{equation}
\bar{u}_0 = 0.0960 \longrightarrow \bar{u}_1 = 0.1062 \longrightarrow \bar{u}_2 = 0.1192 \longrightarrow \bar{u}_3 = 0.1248 \longrightarrow \bar{u}_4 = 0.3480
\end{equation}
monotonically verifying Kaplan et al.'s theoretical prediction \cite{kaplan2015subjective}.
